When to see a dentist

  • Tooth pain that keeps you up at night
  • Gums that bleed when you brush
  • Swelling in your face or jaw
  • Sensitivity to hot or cold that won't go away
  • It's been more than six months since your last checkup

Common questions

Still have a question? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Is root canal treatment painful?

It's done under local anaesthesia, so it should feel similar to a filling. Some soreness for a few days afterwards is normal.

How often should I get my teeth cleaned?

Every six months for most people, or more often if you have gum disease.

At what age can children get braces?

Orthodontists usually assess children around age 7, and most braces are fitted between 10 and 14.

How long do implants last?

With good care, an implant can last 20 years or more.
