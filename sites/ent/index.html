  • A sore throat that keeps coming back
  • A blocked nose for more than ten days
  • Sudden loss of hearing in one ear
  • Hoarseness lasting more than three weeks

Common questions

Still have a question? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Should I clean my ears with cotton buds?

No. Buds push wax deeper and can injure the eardrum. If your ear feels blocked, have it cleaned at a clinic.

When are tonsils removed?

Usually when a child has many throat infections in a year, or when large tonsils cause snoring or pauses in breathing during sleep.

Is sudden hearing loss an emergency?

Yes. Sudden hearing loss in one ear should be seen within 24 to 48 hours, because early treatment improves recovery.

Can allergies cause sinus problems?

Yes. Nasal allergies are a common cause of blocked sinuses, and treating the allergy often helps.
