When to see an eye specialist

  • Blurry vision or trouble reading
  • Sudden loss of vision or flashes of light
  • Red, painful eyes
  • You have diabetes and haven't had an eye check this year
  • Frequent headaches when reading or using screens

Common questions

Still have a question? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Am I suitable for LASIK?

Usually, if you're over 18, your glasses power has been stable for a year and your cornea is thick enough. A pre-LASIK test will confirm it.

How long is recovery after cataract surgery?

Most people see clearly within a few days and return to normal activities within a week.

How often should children have an eye test?

Before starting school, and then every one to two years.

Do I need an eye check if I have diabetes?

Yes, once a year. Diabetes can damage the retina before you notice any symptoms.
