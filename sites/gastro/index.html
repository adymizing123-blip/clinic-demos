Know the signs

When to see a gastroenterologist

  • Heartburn more than twice a week
  • Difficulty swallowing
  • Blood in vomit or stool
  • Yellow eyes or skin
  • Unexplained weight loss or ongoing tummy pain

Ask on WhatsApp
Is endoscopy painful?

Endoscopy is done with sedation or a throat spray. Most people feel only mild discomfort and go home the same day.

Can hepatitis C be cured?

Yes. Modern tablets cure hepatitis C in most people within 12 weeks.

What causes fatty liver?

Excess weight, diabetes and high cholesterol are the most common causes. Losing weight is the main treatment.

Do I need to fast before an endoscopy?

Yes, usually for 6 to 8 hours. The clinic will give you exact instructions.
