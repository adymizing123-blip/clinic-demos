Know the signs

When to see a cardiologist

  • Chest pain or tightness, especially with exertion
  • Breathlessness when climbing stairs
  • A racing or irregular heartbeat
  • Swelling in your feet and ankles
  • A family history of heart disease before 55

When it can't wait

Severe chest pain, or chest pain with sweating, breathlessness or pain spreading to your arm or jaw, can be a heart attack. Call 1122 or go to the nearest emergency department now.

When is chest pain an emergency?

Call 1122 or go to an emergency department if chest pain is severe, lasts more than a few minutes, or comes with sweating, breathlessness or pain spreading to the arm or jaw.

Do I need to fast before my visit?

Not for a consultation. For a cholesterol test, your doctor may ask you to fast for 9 to 12 hours.

What's the difference between an ECG and an echo?

An ECG records your heart's electrical rhythm in a few minutes. An echo is an ultrasound that shows how the heart muscle and valves are working.

At what age should I get my heart checked?

From 40, or earlier if you smoke or have diabetes, high blood pressure or a family history of heart disease.
