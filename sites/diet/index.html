Common questions

Still have a question? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Will I have to stop eating roti and rice?

No. A good plan adjusts portions and pairings instead of cutting out the foods you eat every day.

How fast will I lose weight?

Half a kilogram to one kilogram a week is a safe, lasting pace for most people.

Is a dietitian the same as a nutritionist?

Dietitians have clinical training to manage diet for medical conditions. If you have a health condition, check that your provider is a qualified dietitian.

Can I consult online?

Yes. Most dietitians at this clinic offer video consultations and WhatsApp follow-ups.
