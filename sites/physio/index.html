Common questions

Still have a question? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

No. You can book a physiotherapist directly. Bring any reports or scans you have.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the problem. Many people improve within 6 to 10 sessions.

What should I wear?

Loose, comfortable clothes that let you move and give access to the area being treated.

Can physiotherapy help me avoid surgery?

For many back, knee and shoulder problems, yes. Your physiotherapist will tell you if surgery should be considered.
