When to see a dermatologist

  • Acne that hasn't improved with over-the-counter products
  • Hair falling out in patches or thinning quickly
  • A mole that changes size, shape or colour
  • A rash or itching that lasts more than a week
  • Dark patches spreading on your face

Common questions

Still have a question? Ask us on WhatsApp.

How many sessions does laser hair removal take?

Most people need 6 to 8 sessions, four to six weeks apart. Your dermatologist will advise based on your skin and hair type.

Is PRP safe?

PRP uses your own blood, so allergic reactions are rare. Mild redness or soreness for a day is normal.

Can I use whitening creams?

Avoid creams that weren't prescribed. Many contain strong steroids or mercury that can damage your skin. A dermatologist can recommend safe treatment for pigmentation.

When will I see results for acne?

Most acne treatments take 6 to 8 weeks to show clear improvement. Keep using them as prescribed, even if progress feels slow.
