Know the signs

When to see a neurologist

  • Headaches that are new, severe or getting worse
  • A seizure or fainting spell
  • Numbness or weakness on one side of the body
  • A tremor or slow movement
  • Memory problems affecting daily life

When it can't wait

Sudden face drooping, arm weakness or slurred speech can be a stroke. Call 1122 immediately. Treatment works best in the first few hours.

When is a headache serious?

Get urgent care for the worst headache of your life, a headache with fever and a stiff neck, or a headache after a head injury.

Can epilepsy be controlled?

Most people with epilepsy become seizure-free with the right medicine.

How quickly does a stroke need treatment?

Clot-dissolving treatment works best within 4.5 hours of symptoms starting, so get to hospital immediately.

Do I need an MRI?

Not always. Your neurologist will decide based on your symptoms and examination.
