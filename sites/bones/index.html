When to see an orthopedic surgeon

  • Joint pain that lasts more than two weeks
  • You can't put weight on a leg after a fall
  • Back pain that spreads down your leg
  • Swelling or stiffness in the mornings
  • A joint that locks, clicks or gives way

Common questions

Still have a question? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Do I need an X-ray before my visit?

Not necessarily. The doctor will order the right scan after examining you. Bring any old X-ray or MRI reports you have.

Will I need surgery?

Most bone and joint problems are treated without surgery, using medicine, rest and physiotherapy. Surgery is advised only when these don't work or the injury needs it.

How long does a fracture take to heal?

Most fractures heal in 6 to 12 weeks, depending on the bone and your age.

Should I see an orthopedic surgeon or a physiotherapist for back pain?

See an orthopedic surgeon first if the pain followed an injury, spreads down your leg or comes with numbness. For a muscle strain, a physiotherapist is often enough.
