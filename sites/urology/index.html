Know the signs

When to see an urologist

  • Severe pain in your side or back that comes in waves
  • Burning or pain when passing urine
  • Blood in your urine
  • A weak flow or getting up often at night to urinate
  • Leaking urine

Can kidney stones pass on their own?

Small stones, under about 5 mm, often pass with water and medicine. Larger stones usually need treatment.

How much water should I drink to prevent stones?

Enough to pass about 2.5 litres of urine a day. For most people that means around 3 litres of fluid, and more in summer.

At what age should men get a prostate check?

Usually from 50, or from 45 if there's a family history of prostate cancer.

Do urologists treat women?

Yes. Urologists treat urinary problems in both men and women.
